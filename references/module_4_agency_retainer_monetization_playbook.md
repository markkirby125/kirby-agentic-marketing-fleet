# Module 4: Agency Retainer Monetization & Outreach Playbook

*Source: Zubair Trabzada ("I Built An Entire AI Marketing Team With Claude Code In 16 Minutes")*

The technical audit is not a standalone deliverable—it is a **Trojan Horse customer acquisition asset**. By delivering high-value diagnostic audits for free, operators bypass defensive gatekeepers and convert cold prospects into high-ticket monthly retainers ($2,500–$5,000/mo).

---

## 4.1 The Trojan Horse Outreach Protocol

Traditional agency outreach fails because it pitches generic services (*"We do SEO and Facebook Ads"*). The Trojan Horse protocol leads with immediate, undeniable diagnostic value.

```
[Prospect Identification]
(Med Spas, Roofers, High-Ticket Trades)
       │
       ▼
[/market-audit Execution (2 mins)]
       │
       ▼
[Identify 2 Critical Revenue Leaks]
(e.g., Pricing Conflict + Zero Meta Descriptions)
       │
       ▼
[Send 90s Loom Video + PDF Attachment]
       │
       ▼
[Convert to 3-Tier Retainer ($2.5k-$5k/mo)]
```

### The Outreach Email Script:
```text
Subject: Found a checkout pricing conflict on [Business Name]

Hi [Owner First Name],

I was reviewing your website while doing research on top [Niche] providers in [City], and noticed a critical pricing discrepancy between two of your pages:

On your [Service Name] page, the package is listed at $1,400 (Save $400), but on your Specials page, the exact same package is listed at $1,500 (Save $300).

When prospective clients see two different prices, they hesitate and bounce instead of booking.

I ran a full 6-agent marketing and technical audit on your site and compiled the complete diagnostic report (attached as a PDF). It also uncovered that 100% of your pages are missing custom meta descriptions, causing Google to auto-generate search snippets.

I recorded a quick 90-second video walking through how to fix these two issues immediately: [Loom Video Link]

No pitch—take the PDF and have your web team patch it. But if you'd like us to handle the fixes and execute the 30-day growth plan, let's grab 10 minutes this week: [Booking Link]

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## 4.2 The 3-Tier Agency Retainer Architecture

When prospects book a discovery call, present a structured 3-phase retainer rather than vague hourly billing:

| Retainer Tier | Monthly Investment | Operational Scope | Deliverables |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Foundational Patch** | **$2,000 – $2,500/mo** | Technical SEO & Funnel Hygiene | Fix all pricing conflicts, write 100% custom meta descriptions, deploy `LocalBusiness` / `MedicalBusiness` JSON-LD schema, embed live Google review widget. |
| **Tier 2: Growth & Entity Domination** | **$3,500 – $4,500/mo** | Full Entity Mirroring & CRO | Build dedicated landing pages for every GBP category/service (Caleb Ulku Mirror Image Law), install transparent pricing calculators, eliminate navbar bloat, execute review drip engine. |
| **Tier 3: Market Monopoly** | **$5,000 – $7,500/mo** | Omnichannel AEO & Link Graph | Tier-1 wire press release syndication (Yahoo Finance), high-traffic parasite placement, branded subreddit defense, paid ads bridge landers. |

## 4.3 Client Retention Rules
1. **Never Report Input Metrics**: Never send clients reports detailing "hours worked" or "tasks completed". Report commercial outcomes: conversion rate increases, Map Pack rank radius expansion, and qualified lead volume.
2. **Automate the Monthly Re-Audit**: On the 1st of every month, re-run `/market-audit`, compile the delta PDF showing score improvements (e.g., moving from 64/100 to 88/100), and email it as proof of ongoing value.

## 4.4 The Immutable Client Change Ledger (`seo_changelog.json`)
Every ongoing retainer engagement must maintain a single-variable isolated change ledger following the production schema in `../../kirby-seo-telemetry/SKILL.md` Module 15. Enforce a 90-day evaluation cooldown before scoring intervention success. Never implement simultaneous multi-variable batches ("shotgun SEO"), protecting the client from confounding noise and protecting the retainer from premature churn.
